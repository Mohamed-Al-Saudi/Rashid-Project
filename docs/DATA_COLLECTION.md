# Data collection checklist

## Person
- person fully visible;
- person far away / partly occluded;
- empty room;
- different clothing and poses;
- camera view partly blocked.

## Light
- light ON at night;
- light OFF at night;
- light OFF in daylight;
- light ON in daylight;
- different brightness levels and camera exposures.

## AC
- AC ON and OFF from the same camera position;
- indicator/display visible if available;
- varied ambient lighting and viewing angles.

## Labelling and splitting
- Keep labels consistent.
- Remove blurry/duplicate frames.
- Split by capture session or time period to reduce data leakage.
- Keep a final test set untouched until model selection is complete.
- Obtain permission before collecting identifiable people.
